🔥 Hormonal Belly Weight Loss Plan

Simple daily routine to reduce hormonal fat

🏃‍♀️ 5 Best Exercises

1. Running

• Start with light jogging for 5 mins (warm-up)
• Increase to a steady running pace for 20 mins
• Slow down to a light jog or walk for last 5 mins

2. Squats

• Stand straight, feet shoulder-width
• Bend knees and go down
• Keep back straight
• Come up slowly (10 reps × 3)

3. Plank

• Keep elbows under shoulders
• Body in straight line
• Hold 20–30 seconds
• Repeat 3 times

4. Skipping

• Start slow for 1 min
• Increase speed gradually
• Continue 5–10 mins

5. Cycling

• Cycle at steady speed
• Maintain for 20 mins
• Keep posture straight
